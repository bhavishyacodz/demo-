Good to know

A few clear answers.

Is the ₹99 fee one-time?

This concept uses a one-time ₹99 registration fee. The final service terms should explain exactly what it covers before payments are enabled.

Does registration guarantee a match?

No. Registration or payment cannot guarantee compatibility, a response, or a successful match.

Who can register?

This service is intended for adults aged 18 and above only. Do not submit anyone else's details without their permission.

How will my details be used?

The final privacy notice must explain what information is collected, why it is needed, who can access it, how long it is kept, and how to request deletion.
